## Use case

I often read at night in a dark room. When I get up to go to the bathroom or get a glass of water from the kitchen, I use the reader's frontlight to light the way.

## Proposal

Add a **Flashlight** action to Home on devices with a frontlight. The control center is already crowded, so Home seems a better place.

It would open a white screen at full brightness. A tap, Back, Confirm, or Home would leave it and restore the previous light state and brightness. The menu entry and exit label should be localized.

I have used this in my X4 Pro fork for a while and it works well for this night-reading routine. I would like to offer a focused implementation for CrossPoint.
