## 5. Found

1. **(the judge, major) The grid claimed the long press by the DRAWN tab bar** while the SDK's hit rect is larger
   (`ensureMinTouchRect`: at least 44 px, snapped to the screen edge within 12 px): a long tap in the bottom strip of
   «Сон» was not claimed and slept on the release. Fixed: the band is the SDK's own hit rect; proven by K2 (red on the
   mutant) and L6 (pass).
2. **(the judge, minors)** the grid's flag came from the 90 ms dwell after routing and was never cleared → now set on
   the press edge before routing and cleared on the claim; the return handler wrote render-read state without the lock
   → `RenderLock`; the list path gave no haptic on a long press → `touchAction(true)` as the grid. Documented, not changed:
   a held finger that drifts 28–60 px fires no long press and its release is a tap (the SDK's rule, before Ф5 too) —
   plan 11, risk 5.
3. **(own run) A scripted marker edit turned the four files CRLF** — EXP-0019 again; caught by the diff stat before the
   commit, LF restored and checked with `git ls-files --eol` (EXP-0029).
